---
title: Tax on late fees
category: taxes
summary: States that Ledgerly does not charge tax on late fees and explains how late fees appear in tax reports.
updated: 2025-06-30
---

Ledgerly does not apply tax to late fees. A late fee is a charge for paying late, not a charge for goods or services, so it is outside the tax calculation on the invoice.

## How it looks on the invoice

The late fee is added as its own line after the taxed lines and after the tax summary. The tax shown on the invoice stays the same before and after a fee is added.

For example, an invoice with a $1,200.00 subtotal and $84.50 in tax has a total of $1,284.50. If the invoice is unpaid and the first late fee is added on day 30 (1.5% of 1,284.50), the invoice shows:

| Row | Amount |
| --- | --- |
| Subtotal | 1,200.00 |
| Tax | 84.50 |
| Late fee | 19.27 |
| Total | 1,303.77 |

The late fee is calculated from the outstanding invoice balance excluding previous late fees (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)). Because the balance includes tax, the fee is 1.5% per month on the amount the client owes, tax included.

## Reports

In the [tax summary report](/help/taxes/tax-summary-report), late fees are listed in a separate column titled "Fees, not taxed". They are not part of taxable sales and not part of tax collected.

## What if my local rules differ

Some jurisdictions treat late fees as taxable, and others do not. Ledgerly provides no setting to add tax to fees. If your adviser says you must charge tax on them, you can add a separate line item with a tax rate manually, and turn off automatic late fees for the invoice (see [Late fee settings](/help/billing/late-fee-settings)).

## Fees waived or reduced

If you waive or reduce a fee, no tax adjustment is needed, since none was charged (see [Waive or reduce a late fee](/help/billing/waive-or-reduce-a-late-fee)).

## Tax-inclusive invoices

On tax-inclusive invoices the late fee is also kept outside the inclusive total (see [Tax-inclusive pricing](/help/taxes/tax-inclusive-pricing)).

## Subscription charges

Your Ledgerly subscription has no late fee, so there is nothing to tax. See [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment).
