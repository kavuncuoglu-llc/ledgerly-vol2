title: Tax IDs on invoices
category: taxes
summary: Explains where to enter your own tax ID and your customer's tax ID so they print on invoices.
updated: 2024-12-09
---

Many countries require a tax or VAT identification number on invoices. Ledgerly prints two: yours and, when you enter one, your customer's.

## Your tax ID

1. Open **Settings > Business details**.
2. Enter your **Tax ID** and choose its type, such as VAT number, GST number or EIN.
3. Save.

The number appears in the header of every invoice, credit note and receipt created after you save. Existing invoices keep the number they were created with, because the invoice is a record of what you sent. If you entered a wrong number, void the invoice and issue a corrected one (see [Void an invoice](/help/invoices/void-an-invoice)).

Ledgerly checks only the format of the number, not whether it is registered.

## Your customer's tax ID

Open the customer, choose **Edit** and enter the **Tax ID** under **Tax**. It prints under the customer's address. Entering a customer VAT ID is required for the reverse charge (see [VAT reverse charge](/help/taxes/vat-reverse-charge)).

## Different IDs per workspace

Each workspace has its own business details. If you operate two companies, set the tax ID in each workspace (see [Workspaces](/help/account/workspaces)).

## IDs for tax rates

A tax rate can also have a **Registration number**, for example when you collect state sales tax with a permit number. It is printed under the tax total and is independent of your main tax ID (see [Add tax rates](/help/taxes/add-tax-rates)).

## Tax ID on your Ledgerly subscription invoices

Your Ledgerly bills use the tax ID in **Settings > Subscription > Billing details**, not the one in Business details. They can be different. See [Subscription invoices](/help/billing/subscription-invoices).

## Formats

IDs can contain letters, digits, spaces and hyphens, up to 30 characters. Remove the country prefix if your tax authority does not use one on invoices.
