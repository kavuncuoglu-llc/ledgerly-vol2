---
title: VAT reverse charge
category: taxes
summary: Explains how to mark an invoice as reverse charge so that the buyer, not you, accounts for the VAT.
updated: 2025-04-22
---

Under the reverse charge, a business customer in another country accounts for VAT on your sale instead of you charging it. It is common for services sold between businesses in different EU countries. Ledgerly only applies the printed wording and zero-rating you ask for; whether the reverse charge applies is your decision, so confirm it with your tax adviser.

## Apply it to an invoice

1. Make sure the customer has a **Tax ID** (see [Tax IDs on invoices](/help/taxes/tax-ids-on-invoices)).
2. In the invoice editor, switch on **Reverse charge**.
3. Ledgerly sets the tax on all lines to zero and prints the note "VAT reverse charge: the customer is liable to account for VAT". You can edit the note under **Settings > Taxes**.
4. Save and send.

Both your tax ID and the customer's are printed.

## Apply it automatically

On a customer record, switch on **Always use reverse charge**. New invoices for that customer start with the option on.

## What the client sees

All lines show 0% tax with the label "Reverse charge", and the total equals the net total. There is no VAT amount to pay.

## Reports

Reverse charge sales appear in the [tax summary report](/help/taxes/tax-summary-report) in a "Reverse charge" section, with the net sales and the customer's tax ID, which you can use in your periodic filings.

## Compared with tax-exempt

A tax-exempt customer does not pay tax because of their status (see [Tax-exempt customers](/help/taxes/tax-exempt-customers)). Reverse charge is a property of the transaction, and the customer must report the tax.

## Late fees and credit notes

Late fees are not taxed regardless of the reverse charge (see [Tax on late fees](/help/taxes/tax-on-late-fees)). A credit note against a reverse charge invoice keeps the reverse charge (see [Credit notes](/help/invoices/credit-notes)).

## Limitations

You cannot combine reverse charge with tax-inclusive pricing. Switch off one before turning on the other.
