If the help center does not answer your question, the support team can help.

## Ways to reach us

- **In the app:** open **Help > Contact support**. This attaches your workspace and plan automatically, which speeds things up.
- **Email:** `support@example.com`, from the address on your profile.
- **Phone (Enterprise only):** the urgent line is +1 555 0100 (see [Support hours and escalation](/help/troubleshooting/support-hours-and-escalation)).

Support is available Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 US Eastern, excluding US public holidays.

## What to include

1. The invoice number, such as INV-4471, or the payment date and amount.
2. What you expected and what happened.
3. A screenshot of any error message.
4. For payment questions, a bank reference if your client has one.

Never send passwords, full card numbers or recovery codes. Support will not ask for them.

## Your ticket

Every request creates a ticket with a number like T-10422, and you receive a confirmation email. Reply to that email to add information. The ticket page, under **Help > My tickets**, shows its state:

| State | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| Open | Received, waiting for an agent |
| Waiting on you | We need information from you |
| Escalated | Passed to a specialist |
| Solved | We believe it is fixed. Reply to reopen within 14 days |

## First response times

Starter: within 2 business days. Pro: within 1 business day. Enterprise: within 4 business hours. These are the times to the first reply, not to a final solution.

## What support can and cannot do

Support can explain how Ledgerly works, look at your invoice history and fix problems with the product. Support cannot change an invoice amount or remove a late fee on your behalf, since those belong to your workspace (see [Waive or reduce a late fee](/help/billing/waive-or-reduce-a-late-fee)). Support also cannot see your clients' bank details.

## Before you write

Try [Client says they paid but the invoice is unpaid](/help/troubleshooting/client-says-paid-but-unpaid) and [Unexpected late fee](/help/troubleshooting/unexpected-late-fee), which cover the most common requests.
