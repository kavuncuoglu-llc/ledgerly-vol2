Start with the message you see. It points to the right fix.

## "Incorrect email or password"

1. Check that Caps Lock is off and that your password manager filled the right account.
2. Use the email you signed up with. If you have accounts with two addresses, try the other one.
3. If you are unsure, request a reset link (see [Reset your password](/help/security/reset-password)).

## "Too many attempts" or a countdown

Your profile is locked for 15 minutes after 5 failures. Wait for the countdown to end (see [Account lockout](/help/security/account-lockout)).

## "Invalid code"

The six-digit code comes from your authenticator app and changes every 30 seconds. If it keeps failing, set your phone to automatic time and try the next code. If the phone is lost, use a recovery code (see [Recovery codes](/help/security/recovery-codes)).

## "Email not verified"

Open the confirmation email you received when you signed up. Links last 24 hours. Choose **Resend confirmation** if needed (see [Create your account](/help/getting-started/create-your-account)).

## "Your workspace uses single sign-on"

Sign in through your company identity provider (see [Single sign-on](/help/integrations/single-sign-on)).

## "Workspace is read-only" after signing in

You are signed in, but the subscription needs attention (see [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment)).

## The page does not load or loops back to sign-in

- Clear cookies for the site or use a private window.
- Disable extensions that block cookies.
- Check the status page at `status.example.com`.

## You do not remember which email you used

Search your inbox for emails from `notify@example.com` or `invoices@example.com`. If none exist, contact support. We will not reveal account details until you prove ownership (see [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support)).

## Invited, but no access

The invitation may have expired after 7 days; ask the Owner to resend it (see [Invite team members](/help/account/invite-team-members)).
