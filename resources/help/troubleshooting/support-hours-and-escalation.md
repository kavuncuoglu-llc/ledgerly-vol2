## Support hours

Support is staffed Monday to Friday, 9:00 to 18:00 US Eastern Time, excluding US public holidays. Requests sent outside these hours are answered on the next business day. There is no weekend support, except for Enterprise customers with an urgent issue.

The status page at `status.example.com` shows incidents at any time of day, and we update it as soon as we know of an outage.

## First response targets

| Plan | First response |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 2 business days |
| Pro | 1 business day |
| Enterprise | 4 business hours |

Business hours are those above. A ticket opened on Friday at 17:30 Eastern by a Pro customer receives its first reply by Monday at 17:30 Eastern.

## How to escalate

Escalation hands a ticket to a billing or technical specialist who can look at account data in more detail.

1. Open the ticket from **Help > My tickets**, or reply to the confirmation email.
2. Write "Please escalate" and say why. Examples: a payout is missing, money has left a client's account but is not showing, or you cannot sign in at all.
3. The state changes to Escalated and a specialist replies the same business day.

Tickets about payments and payouts are escalated to the billing specialists automatically after one agent response if the problem is not solved. Tickets about errors that are reproducible are passed to the engineering team.

## Enterprise urgent line

Enterprise customers can call +1 555 0100 for an urgent issue, such as being unable to send invoices or a failing payment flow, at any time. Use it for urgent matters only, then follow up with a ticket so there is a written record.

## What counts as urgent

Anything that stops you from invoicing or being paid. A question about how a feature works is not urgent.

## Tips for faster resolution

Include the invoice number, dates and amounts, and the ticket number in follow-ups (see [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support)). For common problems, try the checklists in [Client says they paid but the invoice is unpaid](/help/troubleshooting/client-says-paid-but-unpaid) first.
