title: Edit a sent invoice

You can edit an invoice after sending it, but changes are limited so your client never sees numbers that quietly differ from what they agreed to.

## What you can change

On a Sent or Overdue invoice with no payments, you can change:

- The customer's contact details and CC recipients.
- The note and the footer text.
- The due date (see below).
- Line items, quantities, prices and tax rates.

When you save, Ledgerly keeps the previous version in the invoice history and offers to send the client an updated copy. The invoice number stays the same.

If a payment has already been recorded, you can change only the note, the footer and the due date. To change amounts after a payment, void the invoice and create a new one, or issue a [credit note](/help/invoices/credit-notes).

## What you cannot change

- The invoice number.
- The customer. If the invoice went to the wrong customer, void it and create a new invoice.
- The currency, once any payment or credit note exists.

## Changing the due date

You can move the due date earlier or later. If you move it into the future on an Overdue invoice, the status returns to Sent, upcoming reminders are recalculated from the new date and the late fee clock restarts. A fee that has already been added is not removed; use [Waive or reduce a late fee](/help/billing/waive-or-reduce-a-late-fee) for that. Moving the date earlier can make the invoice Overdue immediately.

## Paid and Void invoices

These are locked. A Paid invoice can only be refunded or credited, and a Void invoice cannot be reopened. See [Void an invoice](/help/invoices/void-an-invoice).

## Related

Editing a recurring template does not change invoices that were already created (see [Edit a recurring template](/help/recurring/edit-a-recurring-template)).
