New invoices start as drafts. A draft can be changed or deleted freely, does not count toward your monthly limit and is invisible to your client.

## Steps

1. Go to **Invoices** and choose **New invoice**.
2. Select a **Customer**, or type a name to add one on the spot (see [Add a customer](/help/customers/add-a-customer)).
3. Check the **Issue date**. It defaults to today.
4. Choose **Payment terms**. The default is Net 30. The due date is calculated for you (see [Payment terms](/help/invoices/payment-terms)).
5. Add **Line items**: description, quantity, unit price and an optional tax rate. See [Line items and discounts](/help/invoices/line-items-and-discounts).
6. Add a note for the client, such as bank details or a purchase order number. Notes are limited to 2,000 characters.
7. Choose **Save draft**.

Ledgerly assigns the invoice number when you save, using the format `INV-0001` and counting up (see [Invoice numbering](/help/invoices/invoice-numbering)).

## Settings that affect the invoice

- **Currency.** Defaults to the workspace currency. On Pro and Enterprise you can pick another of the 25 supported currencies per invoice.
- **Charge late fee.** On by default. See [Late fee settings](/help/billing/late-fee-settings).
- **Tax inclusive.** Switch on if your prices already include tax (see [Tax-inclusive pricing](/help/taxes/tax-inclusive-pricing)).
- **Minimum amount.** If you set a minimum invoice amount, a smaller total cannot be sent (see [Minimum invoice amounts](/help/invoices/minimum-invoice-amounts)).

## Sending

When the invoice looks right, choose **Send**. Ledgerly checks that the customer has an email address, then moves the invoice to Sent. More in [Send your first invoice](/help/getting-started/send-your-first-invoice).

## Tips

Use **Duplicate** to start from a previous invoice ([Duplicate an invoice](/help/invoices/duplicate-an-invoice)). For work that repeats, set up a [recurring invoice](/help/recurring/recurring) instead.
