If an invoice for a particular month shows as late, work through the checks below. They explain every case, whichever invoice you are looking at.

## The rules that mark an invoice Overdue

An invoice is marked Overdue when all of these are true:

1. Its status was Sent (not Draft, Paid or Void).
2. The due date has passed. Overdue starts at 00:00 workspace time on the day after the due date.
3. A balance remains. A partial payment does not stop the invoice from being Overdue.

The due date is the issue date plus the payment terms. An invoice issued on March 3 with Net 30 terms is due on April 2 and turns Overdue on April 3. If the invoice looks late sooner than you expect, check the issue date and the terms, as described in [Due date and issue date](/help/invoices/due-date-vs-issue-date) and [Payment terms](/help/invoices/payment-terms).

## Why it might look late when it is not

- **Payment not recorded.** The client paid by bank transfer or cheque and nobody recorded it. See [Mark an invoice as paid](/help/invoices/mark-invoice-as-paid).
- **ACH in progress.** Bank transfers take 3 to 5 business days to clear (see [ACH bank transfers](/help/payments/ach-bank-transfers)).
- **Wrong terms on a recurring template.** Check the terms on the [template](/help/recurring/edit-a-recurring-template).
- **Time zone.** The cutoff uses your workspace time zone (see [Time zone and formats](/help/account/timezone-and-formats)).

## Why a late fee was added

A late fee is added only when the invoice is 30 or more days past its due date, with a balance, and it is not Disputed or on a payment plan. An invoice that is 18 days overdue is Overdue but has not reached the day-30 mark. The rate and calculation are in [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees). If a fee appeared that you did not expect, read [Unexpected late fee](/help/troubleshooting/unexpected-late-fee).

## Reminders

Reminders go out at 3, 7 and 14 days past due. A client who got a reminder will also see the Overdue label on the pay page.

## What to do next

Record any missing payment, offer a [payment plan](/help/payments/payment-plans), or mark the invoice [disputed](/help/billing/disputes) if the client disagrees.
