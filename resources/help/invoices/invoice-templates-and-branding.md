Branding settings control how your invoice looks in email, on the pay page and in the PDF. They apply to every invoice in the workspace.

## Customize the look

Go to **Settings > Invoices > Branding** and set:

- **Logo**: PNG or SVG, up to 2 MB, shown at the top of the invoice. A wide logo works best at 600 by 200 pixels.
- **Accent colour**: used for the Pay now button, headings and the status label.
- **Layout**: Classic, Compact or Modern. Compact fits more lines on one page.
- **Footer text**: up to 300 characters, for bank details, registration numbers or thanks.
- **Business details**: legal name, address and tax ID (these come from the workspace, see [Workspaces](/help/account/workspaces)).

A live preview updates as you change values. Select **Save** to apply them.

## Changes affect new and old invoices

Branding is applied when an invoice is displayed or downloaded, so changing the logo updates how past invoices look too. The amounts and wording do not change. If you need an invoice to look the same forever, download the PDF when you send it (see [Send your first invoice](/help/getting-started/send-your-first-invoice)).

## Per-customer language and wording

You can change the labels used on invoices, for example "Invoice" to "Tax invoice", under **Settings > Invoices > Labels**. This applies to all invoices.

## Email appearance

The email that carries the invoice uses the same logo and accent colour. Pro and Enterprise workspaces can also send from their own domain (see [Send from your own domain](/help/integrations/send-from-your-own-domain)) and edit the message text (see [Reminder email templates](/help/reminders/reminder-email-templates)).

## PDF problems

If a logo is cut off in the PDF, resize it to a 3:1 ratio. If the PDF will not download, see [Invoice not sending](/help/troubleshooting/invoice-not-sending).
