Duplicating copies an invoice into a new draft so you do not have to retype line items. It is the quickest way to bill the same work again for a different date or a different customer.

## How to duplicate

1. Open the invoice you want to copy. Any status works, including Paid and Void.
2. Choose **More > Duplicate**.
3. A new draft opens with the next free invoice number.

## What is copied

- Customer (you can change it before saving)
- Line items, quantities, prices and tax rates
- Notes, footer and currency
- Payment terms and the **Charge late fee** setting

## What is not copied

- The invoice number, which is always new
- The issue date, which is set to today
- The due date, which is recalculated from the terms
- Payments, credit notes, reminders and history
- The Disputed badge and any payment plan
- Any late fee that had been added

Because the issue date resets, an old invoice that was Overdue becomes a clean new draft with no fees.

## Duplicating versus recurring

Duplicating is a one-time copy that you review before sending. If you copy the same invoice every month, use [Recurring invoices](/help/recurring/recurring) so Ledgerly does it on schedule.

## Duplicating a Void invoice

This is the usual way to correct a mistake. Void the wrong invoice, duplicate it, fix the details and send the new one. See [Void an invoice](/help/invoices/void-an-invoice). Drafts created by duplication count toward your monthly invoice limit only after they are sent (see [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits)).
