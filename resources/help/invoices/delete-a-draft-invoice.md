---
title: Delete a draft invoice
category: invoices
summary: Explains how to delete a draft and why a sent invoice cannot be deleted.
updated: 2024-08-29
---

Only drafts can be deleted. Once an invoice has been sent, it becomes part of your financial record and you void it instead.

## Delete one draft

1. Open **Invoices** and filter by status **Draft**.
2. Open the draft and choose **More > Delete draft**.
3. Confirm.

The draft is removed permanently. It has no number history to worry about: a draft receives its number only when it is saved, and Ledgerly keeps numbers sequential by reusing the number of a deleted draft only if it was the most recent one.

## Delete several drafts

In the Invoices list, tick the boxes next to the drafts and choose **Delete selected**. You can select up to 100 at a time.

## Why you cannot delete a sent invoice

Deleting a sent invoice would leave gaps in your numbering and make payments and tax reports inconsistent, which many tax authorities treat as a problem. Instead:

- Use [Void an invoice](/help/invoices/void-an-invoice) if the invoice should not exist.
- Use a [credit note](/help/invoices/credit-notes) to reduce what is owed.

## What if the draft came from a recurring schedule

If auto-send is off, each run of a [recurring schedule](/help/recurring/recurring) creates a draft. Deleting that draft removes only that run. The schedule goes on, and the next run still creates its own invoice. To stop future drafts, pause or end the schedule (see [Pause or end a recurring invoice](/help/recurring/pause-or-end-a-recurring-invoice)).

## Can I get a deleted draft back

No. Deleted drafts cannot be restored. If you are unsure, leave the draft in place; drafts do not count toward your invoice limit and they never send reminders or fees.
