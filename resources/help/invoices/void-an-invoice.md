Voiding cancels an invoice that was already sent while keeping a record of it. The invoice stays in your history with the status Void, and its number is never reused.

## How to void

1. Open a Sent or Overdue invoice.
2. Choose **More > Void invoice**.
3. Pick a reason and confirm. The reason is saved in the history and not shown to the client.

If the invoice has partial payments, Ledgerly asks what to do with the money. You can refund the payments (card payments only; see [Refunds](/help/payments/refunds)) or keep them as customer credit on the account (see [Customer credit balance](/help/customers/customer-credit-balance)).

## What happens when an invoice is voided

- The status changes to Void and the balance stops counting as owed.
- Reminders stop, and late fees stop. A fee already added is removed along with the invoice.
- The pay link shows the invoice as cancelled, so the client cannot pay it.
- Recurring schedules are not affected; the schedule keeps creating future invoices.

You cannot reopen a Void invoice. If you voided it by mistake, [duplicate it](/help/invoices/duplicate-an-invoice) and send the copy.

## Void, delete or credit note?

| Situation | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Draft that was never sent | [Delete a draft invoice](/help/invoices/delete-a-draft-invoice) |
| Sent invoice that was wrong or should not exist | Void |
| Sent invoice where you want to reduce the amount and keep it | [Credit note](/help/invoices/credit-notes) |
| Paid invoice | Refund or credit note (a Paid invoice cannot be voided) |

## Reports

Void invoices are excluded from totals, tax reports and the monthly invoice count. If the invoice was already counted for the month, the count does not go down.
