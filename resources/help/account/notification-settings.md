Ledgerly tells you about activity on your invoices. Notification settings control which events reach you and where. They are personal: each team member chooses their own.

## Where to find them

Open **Profile > Notifications**. Each row has two switches, **Email** and **In-app**.

## Events you can choose

| Event | Default |
| --- | --- |
| Invoice viewed by client | In-app only |
| Invoice paid | Email and in-app |
| Payment failed | Email and in-app |
| Invoice became Overdue | In-app only |
| Late fee applied to an invoice | Email and in-app |
| Dispute opened by a client | Email and in-app |
| Recurring invoice created | Email |
| Recurring invoice created as draft | Email and in-app |
| Weekly summary (Mondays) | Email |

## Notifications you cannot turn off

Security and billing messages are always sent to the Owner: sign-in from a new device, password changes, payment problems with your Ledgerly subscription (see [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment)) and messages about closing the workspace. Receipts to your clients for payments made through the pay link are also required (see [Payment receipts](/help/payments/payment-receipts)).

## Notifications to your clients

These are controlled in the workspace, not in your personal settings. The wording of reminders is in [Reminder email templates](/help/reminders/reminder-email-templates), and the timing in [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders).

## Workspaces

Settings apply to all workspaces you belong to. To get a notification for only one workspace, mute the others from the switcher menu with **Mute this workspace**.

## Digest mode

Switch on **Combine into a daily email** to receive one summary at 08:00 in your time zone instead of several emails. Failed payments and disputes are still sent immediately.

## Not getting any

If emails are not arriving, check spam and add `notify@example.com` to your contacts. If it still fails, see [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support).
