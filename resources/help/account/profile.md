Your profile belongs to you as a person and follows you across every workspace you can access. Business details such as the company name and address on invoices live in the workspace instead (see [Workspaces](/help/account/workspaces)).

## Editing your profile

Open the menu in the top right corner, choose **Profile**, and change any of these fields:

- **Full name**: shown to teammates and in activity history.
- **Photo**: a square image up to 2 MB, PNG or JPG.
- **Language**: the language of the Ledgerly interface. It does not change the language of invoices your clients receive.
- **Email**: changing this requires confirmation, covered in [Change your email address](/help/account/change-your-email).

Press **Save** to apply the changes. They take effect immediately.

## Setting your location

Your location is the country or region you work from. Ledgerly uses it to suggest a default currency, a date format and a time zone when you create a new workspace.

1. Open **Profile**.
2. Under **Location**, pick a country and, where asked, a region.
3. Choose **Save**.

Changing your location does not change any existing workspace, invoice or tax rate. If you want to change the currency or time zone of a workspace that already has invoices, see [Default currency](/help/account/default-currency) and [Time zone and formats](/help/account/timezone-and-formats). Location also never adds tax automatically; tax rates are configured separately (see [Add tax rates](/help/taxes/add-tax-rates)).

## Notifications and security

Email notification choices are on their own page, [Notification settings](/help/account/notification-settings). Password and two-factor options are under **Profile > Security**; see [Two-factor authentication](/help/security/two-factor-authentication).

## What clients can see

Your profile name and photo are visible to teammates only. Clients see the business name, logo and reply-to address of the workspace that sent the invoice, not your personal profile.
