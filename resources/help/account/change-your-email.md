The email address on your profile is what you sign in with and where Ledgerly sends security notices. Changing it requires confirmation of both the old and the new address.

## Change it

1. Open **Profile** and choose **Change email**.
2. Enter your current password.
3. Type the new address.
4. Select **Send confirmation**.

Ledgerly sends a link to the new address and a notice to the old one. Open the link within 60 minutes. Until you do, the old address stays active. If you did not request the change, use the notice sent to the old address to cancel it and then [reset your password](/help/security/reset-password).

## What changes

- The address you sign in with
- Where password reset links and security alerts are sent
- Your name in team lists and activity logs, which show the new address

## What does not change

- The email on invoices, reminders and receipts. That is set per workspace under **Settings > Business details** (see [Workspaces](/help/account/workspaces)).
- Billing contacts for the subscription (see [Subscription invoices](/help/billing/subscription-invoices)).
- Customer contacts.

If you want invoices to come from a new reply-to address, change it in the workspace as well.

## Common problems

**The confirmation email does not arrive.** Check spam, and make sure you typed the address correctly. You can request another link, but only one pending change can exist.

**"Email already in use."** Another Ledgerly account uses the address. Sign in to that account and change or delete it first, or use a different address. Two accounts cannot share an address.

**Locked out of the old mailbox.** If you can no longer open the old address, contact support, who will verify your identity (see [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support)).

## Two-factor authentication

Your authenticator app and recovery codes stay the same after an email change (see [Two-factor authentication](/help/security/two-factor-authentication)).
