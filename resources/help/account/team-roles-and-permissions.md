Every person in a workspace has one of four roles. The role controls what they can see and change.

## What each role can do

| Action | Owner | Admin | Biller | Viewer |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| See invoices, customers and reports | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Create and send invoices | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Record payments | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Waive or reduce late fees | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Issue refunds | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Void invoices | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change settings (taxes, branding, late fees) | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Invite and remove team members | Yes | Yes | No | No |
| Change plan and payment method | Yes | No | No | No |
| Close the workspace | Yes | No | No | No |

Viewers can export data only when the Owner switches on **Allow Viewers to export**.

## Change a role

1. Go to **Settings > Team**.
2. Find the person and open the **Role** menu.
3. Choose a new role. The change applies immediately.

Only Owners and Admins can change roles, and only an Owner can make someone else an Admin or Owner.

## Transfer ownership

A workspace has exactly one Owner. To hand over:

1. Make sure the new Owner is already on the team.
2. Choose **Settings > Team > Transfer ownership**.
3. Confirm with your password and two-factor code.

The new Owner needs to add a payment method to keep the subscription (see [Update your payment method](/help/billing/update-payment-method)). You become an Admin.

## Team size limits

Starter allows 1 member, Pro 5 and Enterprise is unlimited. Pending invitations count (see [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits)).
