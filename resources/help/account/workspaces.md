A workspace is the container for one business in Ledgerly. It holds the customers, invoices, payments, settings, team members and subscription for that business. Some people call it an account or a tenant; in Ledgerly the word is workspace.

## What belongs to a workspace

- Business details, logo and invoice numbering
- Customers, invoices, credit notes and payments
- Tax rates, payment terms and late fee settings
- Recurring schedules and reminder settings
- Team members and their roles
- The plan, payment method and subscription invoices (see [Workspace billing](/help/billing/workspace-billing))

## What belongs to you personally

Your sign-in, name, photo, language and security settings are part of your profile and follow you across workspaces (see [Your profile](/help/account/profile)).

## Create another workspace

1. Open the workspace switcher at the top left.
2. Choose **New workspace**.
3. Enter a business name, country and currency.

Starter includes 1 workspace, Pro up to 3 and Enterprise has no limit. New workspaces start on Starter, because the Pro trial is given once per account.

## Switch between workspaces

Use the same switcher. The name of the current workspace is always shown at the top. Notifications from all your workspaces arrive by email with the workspace name in the subject.

Data is never shared between workspaces. A customer in one is not visible in another, and invoice numbers start from their own sequence (see [Invoice numbering](/help/invoices/invoice-numbering)).

## Rename or delete

Owners can rename a workspace under **Settings > Business details**. Deleting is covered in [Close your account](/help/account/close-your-account).

## Team access

Each workspace has its own team. Add someone to one workspace and they cannot see the others (see [Invite team members](/help/account/invite-team-members)).

## Not to be confused with

A workspace is not the same as a customer's portal (see [Customer portal](/help/customers/customer-portal)) or a company record on an invoice.
