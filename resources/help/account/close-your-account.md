Closing a workspace ends your use of it and schedules its data for deletion. Only the Owner can do this.

## Before you close

1. Export what you need: invoices, customers, payments and attachments (see [Export your data](/help/account/export-your-data)).
2. Collect any money owed to you. Pay links stop working once the workspace is closed.
3. Cancel your subscription if you want to stop billing without deleting (see [Cancel your subscription](/help/billing/cancel-your-subscription)). Closing the workspace also cancels it.

## Close the workspace

1. Go to **Settings > Business details**.
2. Scroll to **Close workspace** and choose it.
3. Type the workspace name to confirm and enter your password.

## What happens

- You and your team are signed out of the workspace.
- Recurring schedules stop, reminders and late fees stop, and pay links show a closed message.
- Any unused time on an annual plan is refunded only if it is within 30 days of purchase (see [Subscription refunds](/help/billing/subscription-refunds)).
- LedgerlyPay payouts continue until your balance is zero. Open chargebacks are still handled (see [Chargebacks](/help/payments/chargebacks)).

## The 90-day period

Data is kept for 90 days after closing. During that time the Owner can ask support to restore the workspace. After 90 days, everything is permanently deleted: customers, invoices, payments, attachments and team records. Billing records for Ledgerly's own invoices to you are retained as long as the law requires (see [Data retention](/help/security/data-retention)).

## Closing your personal sign-in

Closing a workspace does not delete your personal profile. If you belong to other workspaces, you can still sign in. To delete your personal profile too, choose **Profile > Delete my profile** once you no longer belong to any workspace.

## If you only want a break

Consider downgrading to Starter (see [Change your plan](/help/billing/change-your-plan)). Late fees and reminders keep running while a workspace is open.

## Clients' view

Clients who open an old invoice link see a closed message and cannot pay. Tell them in advance.
