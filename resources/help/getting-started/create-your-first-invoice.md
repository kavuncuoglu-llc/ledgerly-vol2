This quick start creates a simple invoice with one line. The full reference, including all the options, is in [Create an invoice](/help/invoices/create-an-invoice).

## Build it

1. Choose **Invoices > New invoice**.
2. Pick the customer you created earlier (see [Add your first customer](/help/getting-started/add-your-first-customer)).
3. Leave the **issue date** as today and the **payment terms** as Net 30. The due date is calculated for you: 30 days after the issue date.
4. Under **Line items**, write a description, a quantity and a price. For example "Website design, 1, 1,200.00".
5. If you charge tax, pick a rate in the **Tax** column (see [Add tax rates](/help/taxes/add-tax-rates)).
6. Check the total at the bottom.
7. Choose **Save draft**.

Your invoice now has a number such as `INV-0001` and the status Draft. Nobody has seen it yet, and it does not count toward your monthly limit.

## What the status means

An invoice moves through five statuses: Draft, Sent, Overdue, Paid and Void. Your new invoice is Draft. It becomes Sent when you send it, Overdue the day after its due date if still unpaid, and Paid when the balance reaches zero. See [Invoice statuses](/help/invoices/statuses).

## Things you can ignore for now

Discounts, branding, recurring schedules and late fee settings all have defaults that work. Late fees are on by default and apply only if an invoice is still unpaid 30 days after its due date (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)).

## Preview

Select **Preview** to see the invoice as your client will. If you spot a mistake, edit the draft freely.
