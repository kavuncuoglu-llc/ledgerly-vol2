A draft is private until you send it. Sending emails the invoice to your client and starts the clock on the due date, reminders and late fees.

## Send

1. Open the draft invoice.
2. Choose **Send**.
3. Check the **recipients**. They are the contacts marked Receives invoices on the customer.
4. Adjust the **subject** and **message** if you wish.
5. Leave **Attach PDF** ticked if you want a copy attached as well as the pay link.
6. Select **Send invoice**.

The status changes to Sent. If the customer has no email address, Ledgerly stops and asks you to add one.

## What the client receives

An email from your business name with a link to the invoice page (see [Pay link for clients](/help/payments/pay-link-for-clients)), and a PDF if you attached it. If you have activated online payments, they see a Pay now button.

## Set the issue date properly

The issue date does not change when you send. If your draft is dated a week ago, the client gets less time to pay than you meant. Set the issue date to today before sending (see [Due date and issue date](/help/invoices/due-date-vs-issue-date)).

## What happens next

- You see a Viewed badge when the client opens the invoice.
- If the invoice is unpaid on the day after the due date, it becomes Overdue.
- Reminders go out 3, 7 and 14 days past due (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders)).
- A late fee is added 30 days past due (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)).
- When the client pays online, the invoice becomes Paid by itself. For other payments, see [Mark an invoice as paid](/help/invoices/mark-invoice-as-paid).

## Monthly limit

Sending counts toward your plan's monthly limit: 50 on Starter and 500 on Pro (see [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits)).
