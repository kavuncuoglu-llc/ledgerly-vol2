Short definitions of terms that appear in Ledgerly and its help articles.

## Invoices and money

**Balance.** The amount still owed on an invoice after payments and credit notes. Late fees are separate from the balance used to calculate later fees.

**Credit note.** A document that reduces what is owed on an invoice (see [Credit notes](/help/invoices/credit-notes)).

**Due date.** The last day to pay without the invoice being late (see [Due date and issue date](/help/invoices/due-date-vs-issue-date)).

**Issue date.** The date on the invoice.

**Payment terms.** The number of days after the issue date that payment is due, such as Net 30.

**Status.** One of Draft, Sent, Overdue, Paid or Void (see [Invoice statuses](/help/invoices/statuses)).

**Badge.** A small label next to a status, such as Partial, Disputed, Payment plan or Viewed.

## Collections

**Overdue.** Past the due date with a balance remaining.

**Grace period.** The 30 days after the due date before a late fee is applied.

**Late fee.** A charge of 1.5% of the outstanding balance per month, prorated by day, added after the grace period (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)).

**Reminder.** An email sent at 3, 7 and 14 days past due (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders)).

**Dispute.** A client's objection to an invoice, which pauses reminders and late fees (see [Disputing a charge](/help/billing/disputes)).

**Payment plan.** A schedule of 2 to 6 installments on one invoice (see [Payment plans](/help/payments/payment-plans)).

## Account

**Workspace.** The container for one business, with its own customers, invoices, team and plan (see [Workspaces](/help/account/workspaces)).

**Owner.** The person who pays for and fully controls a workspace.

**Plan.** Starter, Pro or Enterprise.

**LedgerlyPay.** Ledgerly's payment service for cards and bank transfers.

**Recurring invoice.** An invoice that repeats on a schedule (see [Recurring invoices](/help/recurring/recurring)).

**Ticket.** A support request, numbered like T-10422 (see [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support)).
