Every new workspace starts with a 14-day free trial of the Pro plan. You do not need to enter a card.

## What you get

All Pro features and limits: up to 500 customers, 500 invoices a month, 5 team members, 50 recurring schedules, all 25 currencies, customizable reminders, the API and accounting sync. LedgerlyPay is available, and real client payments are processed normally, with the usual fees (see [Payment processing fees](/help/payments/payment-processing-fees)).

Invoices you send during the trial are real. Reminders and late fees run as usual, so if you are only testing, create a test customer with your own email address.

## Days left

The number of trial days remaining is shown in the banner at the top of the workspace. Emails remind the Owner 3 days and 1 day before the trial ends.

## When the trial ends

Choose a plan from **Settings > Subscription** (see [Choose a plan](/help/getting-started/choose-a-plan)). If you do nothing, the workspace becomes read-only: you can view and export everything and record payments, but you cannot send invoices. Your data is kept for 90 days, and picking a plan unlocks the workspace right away. Reminders and late fees continue for invoices that were already sent.

If you pick Starter and your data exceeds its limits, you must remove or archive items first. See [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits).

## Extending the trial

The trial cannot be extended automatically. If you need more time to evaluate Ledgerly, contact support (see [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support)). Enterprise evaluations are arranged with the sales team.

## One trial per account

A trial is given once per account. Additional workspaces you create after the first one start on Starter unless you upgrade them (see [Workspace billing](/help/billing/workspace-billing)).

## Cancelling during the trial

You do not have to cancel because nothing is charged. To remove the workspace entirely, see [Close your account](/help/account/close-your-account).
