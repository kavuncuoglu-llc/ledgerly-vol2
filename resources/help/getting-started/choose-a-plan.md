All plans include invoicing, online payments, payment reminders and late fees. The differences are in volume, team size and automation. The full comparison is in [Plans and pricing](/help/billing/plans-and-pricing) and the numbers are in [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits).

## Questions that decide it

**How many clients and invoices do you have?** Starter covers 25 customers and 50 invoices a month. Pro covers 500 customers and 500 invoices a month. If you are close to either number, choose Pro.

**Do you work with others?** Starter has one user. Pro has 5, which you can use for a bookkeeper or an assistant. Enterprise is unlimited.

**Do you invoice in more than one currency?** Starter works in one currency. Pro supports all 25.

**Do you want to customize reminders?** On Starter the schedule is fixed: 3, 7 and 14 days past due. On Pro you can build your own schedule and edit the emails (see [Customize the reminder schedule](/help/reminders/customize-reminder-schedule)).

**Do you need integrations?** The API, webhooks, accounting sync and bank feeds are in Pro and Enterprise.

**Do you need single sign-on or a custom late fee?** Those are Enterprise.

## Quick guide

| You are | Likely plan |
| --- | --- |
| A freelancer with a handful of clients | Starter, $19 a month |
| A small business with a team or foreign clients | Pro, $49 a month |
| A larger organization with security needs | Enterprise, custom price |

## You can change later

Upgrades are immediate and prorated. Downgrades happen at the next renewal, as long as you are within the lower plan's limits (see [Change your plan](/help/billing/change-your-plan)). Annual billing saves two months (see [Annual or monthly billing](/help/billing/annual-vs-monthly-billing)).

## Try before deciding

Every workspace begins with a 14-day Pro trial, so you can test everything (see [Free trial](/help/getting-started/free-trial)).
