You need an email address and a password to start. No card is required for the 14-day trial.

## Sign up

1. Go to the Ledgerly sign-up page and choose **Create account**.
2. Enter your **full name**, **email address** and a **password**. Passwords need at least 12 characters.
3. Select **Create account**.

Ledgerly sends a confirmation email with a link that stays valid for 24 hours. Open it to verify your address. Until you do, you can look around but cannot send invoices.

If the email does not arrive within a few minutes, check your spam folder and choose **Resend confirmation** on the sign-in screen.

## Create your workspace

After you confirm, Ledgerly asks for:

- **Business name**, printed on invoices
- **Country**, used to suggest a currency, date format and time zone
- **Currency**, which becomes the workspace's default and can be changed later while you have no invoices (see [Default currency](/help/account/default-currency))

Your workspace starts with a 14-day trial of Pro. You become its Owner, which gives you every permission (see [Team roles and permissions](/help/account/team-roles-and-permissions)).

## Secure it now

Open **Profile > Security** and turn on two-factor authentication (see [Two-factor authentication](/help/security/two-factor-authentication)). It takes under a minute, and you will be asked to save ten recovery codes (see [Recovery codes](/help/security/recovery-codes)).

## Joining an existing workspace

If a colleague invited you, use the link in the invitation instead. Ledgerly asks you to set a name and password, and you join their workspace with the role they chose. See [Invite team members](/help/account/invite-team-members).

## Signing in later

Use your email and password. If you forget the password, use **Forgot password** on the sign-in page (see [Reset your password](/help/security/reset-password)).
