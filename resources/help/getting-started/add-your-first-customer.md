You need a customer before you can invoice anyone. This quick start covers the minimum. The complete field list is in [Add a customer](/help/customers/add-a-customer).

## Create the customer

1. Choose **Customers** in the main menu, then **New customer**.
2. Type the customer's **name**, for example "Acme Studios".
3. Add a **contact**: a name and an email address. Make sure **Receives invoices** is ticked, otherwise Ledgerly has nowhere to send invoices and reminders.
4. Save.

That is all you need. You can fill in the address, tax ID and payment terms later.

## Two details that matter later

**The email address.** Invoices, payment reminders and receipts go to the contacts who receive invoices. A typo here is the most common reason a client "never got" an invoice (see [Client did not receive the email](/help/troubleshooting/client-did-not-receive-email)).

**Payment terms.** A new customer uses your workspace default, Net 30. If this client has agreed to shorter terms, set them now on the customer record so that every invoice picks them up automatically (see [Payment terms](/help/invoices/payment-terms)).

## If you already have a customer list

Skip the manual entry and import a CSV with up to 5,000 rows (see [Import customers](/help/customers/import-customers)).

## Limits

During your trial and on Pro you can have up to 500 customers. Starter allows 25. See [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits).
