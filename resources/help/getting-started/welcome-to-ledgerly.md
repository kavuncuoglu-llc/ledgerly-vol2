Ledgerly is a small invoicing product. You create invoices for your customers, send them, and track whether they are paid. It also chases late payers for you, so you spend less time writing awkward emails.

## What you can do

- Create and send invoices, with tax, discounts and your own branding (see [Create an invoice](/help/invoices/create-an-invoice))
- Let clients pay online by card or bank transfer (see [Accepting card payments](/help/payments/accepting-card-payments))
- Send automatic reminders at 3, 7 and 14 days past due (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders))
- Add a late fee of 1.5% per month automatically when an invoice is 30 days past due (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees))
- Repeat invoices on a schedule (see [Recurring invoices](/help/recurring/recurring))
- Export your data and reports for your accountant

## How Ledgerly is organized

Everything lives in a **workspace**, which is one business with its own customers, invoices, settings and team. Most people have one. Learn more in [Workspaces](/help/account/workspaces).

The main menu has **Invoices**, **Customers**, **Payments**, **Recurring**, **Reports** and **Settings**. Help is at the top right, where you can also contact support.

## Your first hour

1. [Create your account](/help/getting-started/create-your-account).
2. Work through the [first-time setup checklist](/help/getting-started/first-time-setup-checklist).
3. [Add your first customer](/help/getting-started/add-your-first-customer).
4. [Create your first invoice](/help/getting-started/create-your-first-invoice) and [send it](/help/getting-started/send-your-first-invoice).

## Plans

New workspaces start with a 14-day trial of Pro. After that you choose Starter ($19 a month), Pro ($49 a month) or Enterprise. See [Free trial](/help/getting-started/free-trial) and [Plans and pricing](/help/billing/plans-and-pricing).
