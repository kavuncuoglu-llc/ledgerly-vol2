Ledgerly works out of the box, but a few settings are easier to set once at the beginning than to fix on invoices later. This checklist takes about ten minutes.

## Business details

1. Open **Settings > Business details** and check the legal name, address and email. They print on every invoice.
2. Add your **Tax ID** if your country requires one (see [Tax IDs on invoices](/help/taxes/tax-ids-on-invoices)).
3. Upload your **logo** under **Settings > Invoices > Branding** (see [Invoice templates and branding](/help/invoices/invoice-templates-and-branding)).

## Money settings

1. Confirm the **default currency** (see [Default currency](/help/account/default-currency)). It is hard to change once you have invoices.
2. Choose your **default payment terms**. The default is Net 30 (see [Payment terms](/help/invoices/payment-terms)).
3. Add your **tax rates** (see [Add tax rates](/help/taxes/add-tax-rates)).
4. Activate **LedgerlyPay** if you want clients to pay online (see [Accepting card payments](/help/payments/accepting-card-payments)).

## Collections settings

The defaults are sensible, but know what they do:

- Automatic reminders go out 3, 7 and 14 days past due (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders)).
- A late fee of 1.5% per month is added 30 days past due. If your contracts do not allow late fees, turn them off now in **Settings > Billing rules > Late fees** (see [Late fee settings](/help/billing/late-fee-settings)).

## Account settings

- Check your **time zone** (see [Time zone and formats](/help/account/timezone-and-formats)), since it decides when an invoice becomes Overdue.
- Turn on [two-factor authentication](/help/security/two-factor-authentication).
- Invite your team, if you have one (see [Invite team members](/help/account/invite-team-members)).

## Then

Add a customer and create your first invoice (see [Add your first customer](/help/getting-started/add-your-first-customer)).

## Not sure about a setting?

Most of them can be changed later and apply to new invoices only. The currency and the invoice numbering prefix are the two to get right the first time (see [Invoice numbering](/help/invoices/invoice-numbering)).
