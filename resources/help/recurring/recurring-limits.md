The number of recurring schedules you can have depends on your plan. The limit counts schedules per workspace.

## Limits by plan

| Plan | Active recurring schedules |
| --- | --- |
| Starter | 3 |
| Pro | 50 |
| Enterprise | Unlimited |

Every schedule can create up to 120 invoices before it ends on its own (see [Recurring intervals and dates](/help/recurring/recurring-intervals-and-dates)).

## What counts as active

A schedule counts toward the limit if it is **Active** or **Paused**. **Ended** schedules do not count. If you need to free a slot for a new schedule, end one you no longer use (see [Pause or end a recurring invoice](/help/recurring/pause-or-end-a-recurring-invoice)).

## Invoice limits still apply

Invoices from a schedule count toward your monthly sent limit: 50 on Starter, 500 on Pro. Three weekly schedules on Starter create about 13 invoices a month, which leaves room for manual invoices. A monthly invoice is counted when it is sent, not when the schedule is created.

When the monthly invoice limit is reached, schedules keep creating invoices as drafts. Ledgerly emails you, and the drafts can be sent in the next cycle or after an upgrade. See [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits).

## When you reach the schedule limit

The **Make recurring** button explains the limit and offers an upgrade. Existing schedules continue to run. After a downgrade, Ledgerly will not allow it until active schedules are within the lower limit (see [Change your plan](/help/billing/change-your-plan)).

## Counting examples

A Starter workspace has two Active schedules, one Paused and one Ended. That is three counted schedules, so no more can be added until one is ended. An Ended schedule can be copied into a new one only if a slot is free.
