---
title: Pause or end a recurring invoice
category: recurring
summary: Explains the difference between pausing and ending a recurring schedule and what happens to invoices already created.
updated: 2025-06-18
---

A recurring schedule has three states: Active, Paused and Ended. You control the state from the **Recurring** page.

## Pause

Choose **Pause** on the schedule when you need a break, for example during a holiday or a contract negotiation.

- No invoices are created while it is paused.
- Runs that fall during the pause are skipped. They do not catch up when you resume.
- A paused schedule still counts as an active schedule toward your plan limit (see [Recurring limits](/help/recurring/recurring-limits)).

Choose **Resume** to continue. Ledgerly shows the next run date before you confirm, and you can edit the dates first.

## End

Choose **End schedule** when the work is finished. An ended schedule cannot be restarted, but you can copy it into a new schedule from its menu. Ending frees a slot against your plan limit.

A schedule also ends by itself when it reaches its end date or its number of occurrences, or the 120-run maximum.

## What happens to existing invoices

Pausing or ending a schedule never changes invoices that already exist. A Sent invoice created last month still follows its own reminders and late fee clock (see [Late fees on recurring invoices](/help/recurring/late-fees-on-recurring-invoices)). Drafts created by earlier runs stay as drafts until you send or delete them.

To stop a single invoice only, void or delete that invoice. The schedule continues. See [Void an invoice](/help/invoices/void-an-invoice) and [Delete a draft invoice](/help/invoices/delete-a-draft-invoice).

## When a customer is archived

If you archive a customer, their schedules are paused automatically (see [Archive a customer](/help/customers/archive-a-customer)).

## When your subscription lapses

Schedules pause when the workspace becomes read-only and need to be resumed after payment. See [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment).

## Notifications

Ledgerly emails the Owner when a schedule ends or is paused automatically, with the reason.
