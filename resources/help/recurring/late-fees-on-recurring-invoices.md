You do not need to add a late fee to a recurring invoice by hand. Late fees are a workspace-wide rule, and every invoice generated by a recurring schedule follows the same rules as any other invoice.

## How to add a late fee to recurring invoices

1. Open **Recurring** and choose the schedule.
2. Select **Edit template**.
3. In the settings panel, switch on **Charge late fee**.
4. Save.

Every invoice created from the next run onward copies this setting. The toggle is on by default because late fees are on by default in the workspace (see [Late fee settings](/help/billing/late-fee-settings)). Starter and Pro workspaces use a fixed rate of 1.5% per month. Enterprise workspaces can set a custom rate for the template.

There is no fee on the template itself. The fee only appears on a generated invoice if that invoice goes unpaid.

## When the fee is added

Each generated invoice has its own clock. A late fee is applied automatically 30 days after that invoice's due date, unless it is Disputed or on a payment plan. An unpaid invoice from January does not affect February's invoice. See [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees) for how the 1.5% is calculated and the example.

## Example

A monthly invoice of $500.00 on Net 30 terms is created on January 1 and is due January 31. The client does not pay. On March 2, which is 30 days after the due date, a fee of $7.50 is added (1.5% of $500.00). The February invoice, created on February 1, has its own separate clock.

## Turning fees off for one schedule

Switch off **Charge late fee** on the template. Existing invoices keep the setting they were created with, so turn it off on those individually if needed.

## Fees do not create new recurring runs

A late fee is never turned into a new recurring invoice. It appears as a Late fee line on the invoice that is overdue.
