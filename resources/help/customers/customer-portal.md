The customer portal is a page, hosted by Ledgerly, where one of your clients can see everything they owe you and everything they have paid. It is available on all plans.

## Turn the portal on

1. Go to **Settings > Customer portal** and switch on **Enable portal**.
2. Open a customer and, in the **Contacts** card, tick **Portal user** for the people who should have access (see [Customer contacts and CC](/help/customers/customer-contacts-and-cc)).
3. Choose **Send portal link**.

The portal link is emailed to the portal users. It is a sign-in link that works for 7 days and can be requested again from the portal page. No password is needed.

## What clients can do

- See all invoices with their status labels (see [Invoice statuses](/help/invoices/statuses))
- Pay one or several open invoices in a single payment, if online payments are on
- Download invoice PDFs and receipts
- Start a dispute on an invoice (see [Disputing a charge](/help/billing/disputes))
- See their statements, if you use them (see [Customer statements](/help/customers/customer-statements))

They cannot edit invoices, see your notes or see other customers.

## Late fees in the portal

Late fees appear on the invoice line as a separate Late fee row, with the date they started. Clients can see the calculation by hovering over the amount, which shows the rate and number of days. The explanation uses the rules in [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees).

## Branding

The portal uses the logo and accent colour from your invoice branding (see [Invoice templates and branding](/help/invoices/invoice-templates-and-branding)).

## Pay link or portal

Each invoice email has a direct pay link for that one invoice (see [Pay link for clients](/help/payments/pay-link-for-clients)). The portal is for a client with several invoices who wants one place to look.

## Turn it off

Untick **Portal user** for a contact to remove their access immediately. Switching off **Enable portal** disables it for everyone, but pay links keep working.
