A customer is the person or company you invoice. You need one before you can send an invoice, and the record keeps their contacts, terms, history and settings together.

## Add one customer

1. Go to **Customers** and choose **New customer**.
2. Enter the **Name**. For a company, use the company name and add people as contacts.
3. Add at least one **Contact** with an email address. Tick **Receives invoices** for the contact who should get invoices and reminders.
4. Fill in the **Billing address**. It is printed on invoices.
5. Optional: set the customer's **Currency**, **Payment terms** and **Tax** settings (see [Edit customer details](/help/customers/edit-customer-details)).
6. Save.

You can also add a customer on the fly from the invoice editor by typing a new name in the **Customer** field.

## Fields

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Up to 120 characters, must be unique in the workspace |
| Email | Required to send invoices |
| Billing address | Printed on the invoice |
| Reference | Optional internal ID, such as an account number |
| Tax ID | Optional (see [Tax IDs on invoices](/help/taxes/tax-ids-on-invoices)) |
| Notes | Internal, never shown to the customer |

## Limits

Starter allows 25 customers, Pro 500 and Enterprise is unlimited. Archived customers do not count (see [Archive a customer](/help/customers/archive-a-customer)). When you hit the limit the New customer button explains it and offers an upgrade, as described in [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits).

## Duplicates

If you enter a name that already exists, Ledgerly warns you. To combine records that were created twice, see [Merge duplicate customers](/help/customers/merge-duplicate-customers).

## Many customers at once

Use the CSV import (see [Import customers](/help/customers/import-customers)).

## Next step
