summary: Explains what archiving a customer does, what it does not do to unpaid invoices and how to restore a customer.

Archiving hides a customer you no longer work with, without deleting any financial records. It also stops them counting toward your plan's customer limit.

## Archive

1. Open the customer.
2. Choose **More > Archive**.
3. Review the warnings, then confirm.

An archived customer disappears from the Customers list and from the customer picker on new invoices. You can still find them by turning on **Show archived** in the Customers list or in global search.

## What happens to their data

- All invoices, payments and credit notes stay in your records and in reports.
- Draft invoices stay as drafts.
- Recurring schedules for the customer are paused (see [Pause or end a recurring invoice](/help/recurring/pause-or-end-a-recurring-invoice)).
- Customer portal access is turned off.

## Unpaid invoices keep going

Archiving does not cancel anything that is already running. If the customer has Sent or Overdue invoices, those invoices keep their reminders and late fees. Reminders still go out at 3, 7 and 14 days past due, and a late fee is still added 30 days past due (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)). If you do not want that, void the invoices, pause reminders or mark them as disputed first.

## Restore

Open the archived customer and choose **Restore**. The customer returns to the list immediately. Restoring checks your customer limit, so a Starter workspace with 25 active customers has to archive another one first. Recurring schedules stay paused, and you resume them manually.

## Delete instead?

Customers with invoices cannot be deleted, because the invoices need to stay on record. A customer with no invoices can be deleted from **More > Delete**.
