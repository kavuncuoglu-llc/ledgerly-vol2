A statement is a single document listing everything a customer owes you, including invoices, payments and credits for a period. It is different from an invoice and does not ask the customer to pay a new amount.

Statements are available on Pro and Enterprise.

## Generate a statement

1. Open the customer and choose **More > Statement**.
2. Choose a type:
   - **Outstanding**: only open invoices, grouped by age (current, 1 to 30, 31 to 60, 61 and more days past due).
   - **Activity**: all invoices, payments and credit notes in a date range.
3. Choose the period or an "as of" date.
4. Select **Preview**, then **Download PDF** or **Email statement**.

The statement shows invoice numbers, issue dates, due dates, amounts, payments, late fees and the balance. Late fees are listed on the invoice line they belong to and are not separate.

## Monthly statements

To send statements automatically, open **Settings > Notifications > Statements** and switch on **Monthly outstanding statement**. They go out on the first business day of the month to customers who have at least one open invoice. Customers can be excluded from the customer record.

## When to use a statement

- A client with several overdue invoices who asks "how much do I owe?"
- Year-end reconciliation
- A short summary alongside a reminder

It does not replace the reminders at 3, 7 and 14 days past due (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders)), and sending a statement does not count as a reminder.

## Statements compared with the customer portal

A statement is a snapshot. The portal is live, and the client can pay from it (see [Customer portal](/help/customers/customer-portal)).

## Currencies

A statement covers one currency at a time. If a customer has invoices in two currencies, generate two statements.

## Questions on balances

The balance on a statement includes late fees but not unissued drafts. For an overdue balance question, see [Why an invoice is marked overdue](/help/invoices/why-invoice-marked-overdue).
