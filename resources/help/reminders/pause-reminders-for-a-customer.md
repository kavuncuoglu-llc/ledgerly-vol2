---
title: Pause reminders for a customer
category: reminders
summary: Shows how to stop automatic reminders for one customer without affecting other customers or late fees.
updated: 2025-03-25
---

Sometimes you want one client to stop receiving automatic emails, for example while you negotiate or when they pay on a fixed schedule you have agreed on. You can pause reminders per customer.

## Pause

1. Open the customer record.
2. In the **Reminders** card, choose **Pause reminders**.
3. Pick **Until a date** or **Until I turn them back on**.
4. Save.

The customer's name gets a small bell icon with a slash in the Customers list.

## What stops and what continues

| Item | While paused |
| --- | --- |
| Automatic reminders at 3, 7 and 14 days | Stopped |
| Upcoming-due reminders | Stopped |
| Manual reminders you send | Still possible |
| Late fees | Continue as usual |
| Overdue status | Continues |
| Recurring invoices | Continue to be created and sent |
| Late fee notice email | Still sent when a fee is first added |

The most important point is that pausing reminders does not pause late fees. If you also want to stop fees for the client, switch on **Never charge late fees** on the customer, or mark an invoice as disputed. See [Late fee settings](/help/billing/late-fee-settings) and [Disputing a charge](/help/billing/disputes).

## Reminders that were skipped

Reminders that would have been sent during the pause are skipped. When you resume, Ledgerly does not send them late. It picks up with the next reminder on the schedule, if any is still due in the future.

## Pausing one invoice

To pause reminders on a single invoice rather than a customer, open the invoice and use **More > Pause reminders**. This is helpful when the client has several invoices and only one is in question.

## Teammates

Owners, Admins and Billers can pause reminders. The action is recorded in the customer history.
