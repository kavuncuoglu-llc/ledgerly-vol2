A manual reminder is an email you trigger yourself. It is useful when you want to nudge a client sooner than the automatic schedule, or when the client lost the original email.

## Send one

1. Open a Sent or Overdue invoice.
2. Choose **Send reminder**.
3. Review the recipients, subject and message. The message starts from the template for the invoice's stage (see [Reminder email templates](/help/reminders/reminder-email-templates)). You can edit it for this send only.
4. Choose **Send**.

The email includes the invoice PDF link and a Pay now button, just like automatic reminders.

## Limits

- One manual reminder per invoice per 24 hours.
- Available on all plans.
- Not available on Draft, Paid or Void invoices.
- Not blocked by a customer's paused reminders (see [Pause reminders for a customer](/help/reminders/pause-reminders-for-a-customer)).

If an invoice is Disputed or on a payment plan, Ledgerly asks you to confirm, because the client may not expect it.

## Manual and automatic reminders together

Manual reminders do not replace automatic ones. The automatic reminders at 3, 7 and 14 days past due still go out on schedule (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders)). If you sent a manual reminder within the previous 24 hours of a scheduled one, Ledgerly still sends the scheduled one, so check the history to avoid emailing a client twice in a day.

## Seeing what was sent

Open the invoice and scroll to **Activity**. Every reminder, automatic or manual, shows the date, recipients and whether the client opened it.

## Reminders for many invoices

Select several overdue invoices in the Invoices list and choose **Remind selected**. Ledgerly sends one email per customer, which lists their selected invoices. The 24-hour limit applies to each invoice.

## Tips

If the client says they never received it, see [Reminder delivery problems](/help/reminders/reminder-delivery-problems).
