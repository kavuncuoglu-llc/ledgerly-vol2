Your workspace data belongs to you. This page describes how long Ledgerly keeps it and when it is removed.

## While your workspace is open

Everything is kept: customers, invoices, payments, credit notes, attachments and activity history. Ledgerly does not delete invoices automatically, even old ones. Deleted drafts are removed immediately (see [Delete a draft invoice](/help/invoices/delete-a-draft-invoice)).

Backups of the system are kept for 35 days and are used only to recover from failures, not to restore individual records on request.

## After a plan ends or payment fails

The workspace is read-only, but nothing is deleted (see [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment) and [Cancel your subscription](/help/billing/cancel-your-subscription)). Data is kept for 90 days after a cancellation takes effect.

## After you close a workspace

Data is kept for 90 days, then permanently deleted. During those 90 days the Owner can ask support to restore it (see [Close your account](/help/account/close-your-account)).

## What may be kept longer

- Ledgerly's own invoices to you (the `LDG-` invoices) for 7 years, as accounting law requires.
- Payment processing records for LedgerlyPay, for the period required by financial regulators.
- Security logs of sign-ins for 12 months.

## Customer data

You decide what to store about your clients. Ledgerly processes it on your behalf. If a client asks you to delete their data, delete or anonymize the customer, which is possible only if they have no invoices (see [Archive a customer](/help/customers/archive-a-customer)). For customers with invoices, you may need to keep records for tax reasons, so consult your adviser.

## Personal data requests

To obtain or delete the personal data we hold about you as a user, email `privacy@example.com` from the address on your profile. We reply within 30 days.

## Export first

You can download a complete copy at any time (see [Export your data](/help/account/export-your-data)).

## Security measures

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. See [Fake emails and phishing](/help/security/fake-emails-and-phishing) for how to reach us about security concerns.
