Recovery codes are one-time passwords that let you sign in when you cannot use your authenticator app, for example if your phone is lost, broken or reset.

## What you get

When you turn on two-factor authentication, Ledgerly shows ten recovery codes, each eight characters long. Each code works once. After you use one, it is crossed off the list.

## Store them safely

- Print the page or save it in a password manager.
- Do not keep them only on the phone that holds your authenticator app.
- Do not email them to yourself or store them in a shared document.

Ledgerly does not keep a readable copy of the codes. If you lose the list, you cannot see it again, but you can generate a new one.

## Use a code to sign in

1. Enter your email and password.
2. On the code screen choose **Use a recovery code**.
3. Type one unused code.

You are signed in. Then set up your authenticator app again, because recovery codes are a stopgap (see [Two-factor authentication](/help/security/two-factor-authentication)).

## Generate new codes

Go to **Profile > Security > Recovery codes** and choose **Generate new codes**. You need to enter your password and a current code. All old codes stop working at once, even the ones you have not used. Generate a new list when you have used seven or more of the ten.

## Running out

Ledgerly warns you by email when only two codes remain.

## No codes and no phone

Contact support. Because recovery of an account is a security risk, you will be asked to prove ownership, for example by confirming recent invoice numbers and the last four digits of the card on the subscription. See [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support). Allow up to two business days.
