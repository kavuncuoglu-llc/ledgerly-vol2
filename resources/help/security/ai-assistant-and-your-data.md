Ledgerly's support assistant is an AI. It is not a person, and every conversation with it says so. You can ask for a person at any time: use the link at the top of the chat.

## What the assistant reads

To answer you, the assistant reads your question, the part of the conversation so far that it needs, and the help articles and records of your own workspace that are relevant, such as the invoice you asked about. It never reads another customer's records.

## Which models see it

Most answers are written by models that Ledgerly runs on its own servers, so the text of your question stays inside Ledgerly. Some questions are sent to a model run by an outside provider. Before that happens, names, email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers and similar personal details are replaced with placeholders, and the provider is one that has agreed not to keep the text or use it to train its models. Workspaces that have chosen a data region only use models in that region.

## Training

Conversations are not used to train or fine-tune any model unless your workspace owner has turned that setting on, and a person in your workspace can exclude their own conversations even then. Turning the setting off stops future use; ask a person on the support team if you want past use removed.

## Deleting your conversations

You can ask for your conversations to be deleted. Ledgerly removes them from the app, its search indexes, its logs and any training sets built from them, and tells you when it is done. See [Data retention](/help/security/data-retention).
