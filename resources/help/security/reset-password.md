---
title: Reset your password
category: security
summary: Shows how to change your password when signed in and how to recover access when you have forgotten it.
updated: 2025-03-12
---

To reset your password, open Settings and go to Profile > Security > Password if you can still sign in. If you cannot, use the link on the sign-in page.

## If you are signed in

1. Open **Profile > Security**.
2. Choose **Change password**.
3. Enter your current password and a new one, twice.
4. Select **Update password**.

Passwords must have at least 12 characters. Ledgerly rejects passwords that appear in known breach lists. Other devices are signed out when you change it.

## If you forgot it

1. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password**.
2. Enter your email address.
3. Open the email titled "Reset your Ledgerly password" and follow the link.
4. Set a new password.

The link is valid for 60 minutes and works once. If it expires, request another. The email always arrives from `security@example.com`; check spam if it does not appear. For safety, Ledgerly shows the same message whether or not the address has an account.

## Two-factor authentication

After a reset you still need your second factor to sign in. If you lost your phone, use a recovery code (see [Recovery codes](/help/security/recovery-codes)).

## Too many attempts

After 5 failed sign-in attempts, the account is locked for 15 minutes. A password reset does not bypass the lock. See [Account lockout](/help/security/account-lockout).

## After a reset

You will receive an email confirming the change. If you did not request it, contact support immediately and see [Fake emails and phishing](/help/security/fake-emails-and-phishing). Review [active sessions](/help/security/active-sessions) to sign out unknown devices.

## Single sign-on users

If your workspace uses single sign-on, reset your password with your company's identity provider instead (see [Single sign-on](/help/integrations/single-sign-on)).
