LedgerlyPay lets clients pay an invoice by credit or debit card from the pay link in the invoice email. When a payment succeeds the invoice updates automatically, with no need to record it by hand.

## Set up LedgerlyPay

1. Go to **Settings > Payments** and choose **Activate LedgerlyPay**.
2. Enter your business type, legal name, address and tax ID.
3. Add the bank account where payouts should go.
4. Verify the owner's identity by uploading a photo ID. Verification usually takes less than one business day.

Until verification finishes you can send invoices, but the Pay now button is hidden. Cards are accepted in all 25 supported currencies; the client pays in the currency of the invoice.

## Which cards

Visa, Mastercard, American Express and Discover. Debit and prepaid cards from these networks work too. Cards are charged at the moment the client confirms, and the funds are paid out to your bank account on the schedule in [Payout schedule](/help/payments/payout-schedule).

## Fees

The fee is 2.9% plus $0.30 per successful card transaction. It is deducted from the payout, not from the invoice, so the client always pays the invoice total. Details and examples are in [Payment processing fees](/help/payments/payment-processing-fees).

## Switching card payments off for one invoice

On the invoice, clear **Allow online payment**. The Pay now button disappears for that invoice, and the client can still see the bank details you put in the note. This is useful for large amounts, where the fee would be significant.

## If a card fails

The client sees the reason from their bank and can try another card. You receive a notification, and the invoice stays unpaid. See [Failed payments](/help/payments/failed-payments).
