Payment processing fees are charged by LedgerlyPay on payments your clients make through the pay link. They are separate from your Ledgerly plan price and from late fees.

## Fee table

| Payment type | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Card | 2.9% + $0.30 per successful payment |
| ACH bank transfer | 0.8%, capped at $5.00 |
| Offline payment you record | No fee |
| Currency conversion at payout | 1% |
| Returned ACH payment | $4.00 |
| Chargeback | $15.00 per dispute |

Fees are the same on every plan. Enterprise customers can negotiate custom rates.

## Examples

A client pays a $500.00 invoice by card. The fee is 2.9% of $500.00, which is $14.50, plus $0.30, for $14.80 in total. You receive $485.20.

The same invoice paid by ACH costs 0.8% of $500.00, which is $4.00, so you receive $496.00.

A $2,000.00 invoice by ACH would be 0.8%, or $16.00, but the cap limits it to $5.00, so you receive $1,995.00.

## Late fees are included in the amount

If an invoice has a $15.00 late fee, the client pays the invoice total including it, and the processing fee is calculated on the full amount paid. The late fee itself is not processed separately. See [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees).

## Refunds

Processing fees are not returned when you refund a payment. See [Refunds](/help/payments/refunds).

## Passing the fee on

Ledgerly does not add a surcharge for you. You can add a line item such as "Card processing" yourself (see [Line items and discounts](/help/invoices/line-items-and-discounts)). Check the card network rules and local law first, since surcharges are restricted in some places.

## Where to see the fees

Each payout lists fees per payment. See [Payout schedule](/help/payments/payout-schedule).
