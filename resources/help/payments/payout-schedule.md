A payout is the transfer from LedgerlyPay to your bank account. It contains card and ACH payments from your clients, after fees.

## Timing

Payouts run every business day. Each payment is paid out 2 business days after it is captured. A card payment made on Monday arrives in your account on Wednesday, subject to your bank's own processing time. ACH payments are paid out 2 business days after they clear, so about 5 to 7 business days from when the client submitted them.

Weekends and US bank holidays are skipped, so a payment made on Friday is paid out on Tuesday.

## Minimum payout

The minimum payout is $10. If your balance is less than that, it carries over and is paid out together with the next payments.

## Reading a payout

Open **Payments > Payouts**. Each payout lists:

- The payments included, with the invoice number
- Fees deducted
- Refunds subtracted
- Any chargeback amounts and fees

The total matches the deposit on your bank statement. Download a CSV of a payout to reconcile it with your books.

## Changing the bank account

Go to **Settings > Payments > Bank account**. For safety, a new account is verified with two small deposits, and payouts to the old account continue until verification finishes. Only the Owner can change the account.

## Currencies

Payouts are made in your bank account's currency. If an invoice was paid in another currency, it is converted at the day's rate when it is paid out, with a 1% conversion fee.

## Delays

A payout can be held if your identity verification is incomplete, if there is an open chargeback or if the bank rejects the transfer. See [Chargebacks](/help/payments/chargebacks) and [Payment stuck pending](/help/troubleshooting/payment-stuck-pending).

Fees are explained in [Payment processing fees](/help/payments/payment-processing-fees).
