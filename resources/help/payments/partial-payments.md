title: Partial payments
category: payments
summary: Explains what happens to an invoice, its reminders and its late fee when a client pays only part of the balance.
updated: 2025-01-30
---

A partial payment is any payment smaller than the outstanding balance. It can come through the pay link, which lets the client choose a custom amount, or from a payment you record yourself.

## Allowing partial payments

Partial payments through the pay link are on by default. To prevent them, clear **Allow partial payment** on the invoice or in **Settings > Payments**. The client must then pay the whole balance.

## What you see

The invoice keeps its current status, either Sent or Overdue, and gains a Partial badge. The balance shown is the amount still owed. The invoice becomes Paid only when the balance reaches zero.

## Reminders

Automatic reminders keep going on schedule, at 3, 7 and 14 days past due, and show the remaining balance rather than the original total (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders)). If you agreed on a schedule with the client, use a [payment plan](/help/payments/payment-plans) instead, which pauses reminders and fees.

## Late fees

A late fee is based on the outstanding balance, so a partial payment lowers the fee for the days after it. The fee that was already added stays. Example: an invoice of $1,000.00 is 40 days past due with $20.00 in fees. The client pays $600.00. From the next night, the daily fee is 0.05% of the remaining $400.00 invoice balance, which is $0.20 a day. Fees never compound, so earlier fees are not part of the base and are not recalculated. The full rules are in [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees).

Payments are applied to the invoice amount first and to late fees last.

## Overpayments

If a client pays more than the balance, the extra amount becomes customer credit (see [Customer credit balance](/help/customers/customer-credit-balance)).

## Recording

For partial payments received outside Ledgerly, see [Mark an invoice as paid](/help/invoices/mark-invoice-as-paid).
