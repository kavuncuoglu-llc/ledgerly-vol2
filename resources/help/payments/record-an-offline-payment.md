An offline payment is any payment that did not go through the Ledgerly pay link. Recording it keeps your balances, reminders and reports accurate.

## Record a payment from the Payments page

1. Open **Payments** and choose **Record payment**.
2. Select the **Customer**. Their open invoices are listed.
3. Enter the **Amount** and **Date received**.
4. Pick the **Method**: bank transfer, cash, cheque or other.
5. Tick the invoices it covers. Ledgerly applies the money to the oldest invoice first. You can change the split by editing the amounts.
6. Save.

One payment can cover several invoices, and any amount left over is kept as customer credit (see [Customer credit balance](/help/customers/customer-credit-balance)).

If you only want to pay off one invoice, use the shortcut on the invoice page, described in [Mark an invoice as paid](/help/invoices/mark-invoice-as-paid).

## What changes when you save

- Invoices paid in full become Paid and stop receiving reminders and late fees.
- Partly paid invoices show a Partial badge (see [Partial payments](/help/payments/partial-payments)).
- The client receives a receipt unless you clear **Email receipt**.

## Fees

Ledgerly does not charge a processing fee on offline payments.

## Refunding an offline payment

Ledgerly cannot move money for payments it did not process. To return the money, refund it yourself outside Ledgerly, then open the payment and choose **Record refund**. The invoice goes back to Sent or Overdue, or you can issue a [credit note](/help/invoices/credit-notes) instead. Refunds of card and ACH payments made through Ledgerly are covered in [Refunds](/help/payments/refunds).

## Matching bank statements

Pro and Enterprise workspaces can connect a bank feed to suggest matches automatically. See [Bank feeds](/help/integrations/bank-feeds).

## Editing and deleting

Open the payment to change the date, method or reference, or choose **Delete payment** to remove it completely.
