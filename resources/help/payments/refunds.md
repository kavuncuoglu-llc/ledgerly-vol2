A refund returns money to a client who paid you through LedgerlyPay. If you are looking for a refund of your Ledgerly subscription, see [Subscription refunds](/help/billing/subscription-refunds).

## Refund rules

- Refunds are available for card and ACH payments made through the Ledgerly pay link.
- You can refund within 90 days of the payment. After that, send the money back yourself and record it manually.
- Refunds can be full or partial. You can refund several times until the full amount is returned.
- The money returns to the original card or bank account in 5 to 10 business days.
- Processing fees are not returned to you.

## How to refund

1. Open the invoice and go to the **Payments** section.
2. Next to the payment, choose **Refund**.
3. Enter the amount, up to the amount paid and not yet refunded.
4. Choose a reason: duplicate, requested by client, billing error or other.
5. Confirm. Only Owners and Admins can refund.

The refund is subtracted from your next payout. If you do not have enough balance, it is taken from the next payments that arrive.

## What happens to the invoice

After a full refund, the invoice goes back to Sent or Overdue, depending on the due date, and its balance reappears. Reminders and the late fee clock start again from that point. If you do not want to chase the client, void the invoice or issue a credit note (see [Credit notes](/help/invoices/credit-notes)).

## Refunding a late fee

If a client paid a late fee and you decide it should not have applied, refund the amount as a partial refund, then waive the fee on the invoice. See [Waive or reduce a late fee](/help/billing/waive-or-reduce-a-late-fee).

## Offline payments

For payments recorded by hand, see [Record an offline payment](/help/payments/record-an-offline-payment).

## Refund compared with chargeback

A refund is something you start. A chargeback is started by the client's bank. See [Chargebacks](/help/payments/chargebacks).
