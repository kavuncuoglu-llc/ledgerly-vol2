A receipt confirms to your client that you received their money. Ledgerly creates one for every payment, whether it came through the pay link or you recorded it yourself.

## When receipts are sent

- **Pay link payments**: the client gets an email receipt within a minute of a successful payment.
- **Recorded payments**: a receipt goes out when you save the payment, unless you clear **Email receipt**.
- **Partial payments**: each payment has its own receipt that shows the remaining balance.

The receipt shows the invoice number, the amount, the payment method, the date and the balance after the payment. Card receipts show only the last four digits of the card.

## Resend a receipt

Open the invoice, go to **Payments**, choose the payment and select **Resend receipt**. You can change the email address before sending. Receipts go to every contact marked Receives invoices unless you select specific recipients.

## Download a receipt

Choose **Download PDF** on the same screen. A receipt PDF carries your logo and footer like the invoice (see [Invoice templates and branding](/help/invoices/invoice-templates-and-branding)).

## Receipts compared with paid invoices

When an invoice becomes Paid, Ledgerly also marks the invoice PDF with a Paid stamp. The stamp is on the invoice, while a receipt is a separate document for one payment. A client who paid in several installments will have several receipts and a single paid invoice.

## Customizing the text

Edit the receipt message under **Settings > Notifications > Receipt email**. You can add a thank-you line and your support contact. Receipt emails cannot be switched off for payments made through the pay link, because card networks require a confirmation.

## Missing receipts

If a client did not get a receipt, check the email address on the customer record and the spam folder. See [Client did not receive the email](/help/troubleshooting/client-did-not-receive-email).
