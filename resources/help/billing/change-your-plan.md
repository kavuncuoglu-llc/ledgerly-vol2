Only the workspace Owner can change the plan. Open **Settings > Subscription** and choose **Change plan**.

## Upgrading

Pick a higher plan and confirm. The new features and limits are available immediately.

Ledgerly credits the unused time on your current plan and charges the difference for the rest of the period. For example, if you move from Starter ($19 a month) to Pro ($49 a month) exactly halfway through a monthly period, you pay about $15 now (half of the $30 difference), and the next renewal is $49. The exact amount is shown before you confirm.

If you upgrade from monthly to a higher annual plan, the credit from the monthly plan is applied to the annual price.

## Downgrading

A downgrade takes effect at your next renewal, not immediately. Until then you keep everything you have paid for. Before you can schedule it, your usage must fit the lower plan: customers, team members, workspaces and active recurring schedules (see [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits)). Ledgerly lists what you need to remove or archive.

Features that disappear on downgrade:

- From Pro to Starter: custom reminder schedules revert to the fixed 3, 7 and 14 day schedule, and the API and integrations stop working. Your keys and settings are kept for 90 days in case you upgrade again.
- Late fee settings stay at 1.5%.

Enterprise plans are changed by contacting support; they cannot be changed from the Subscription page.

## Cancelling a scheduled change

While a downgrade is waiting, a banner appears on the Subscription page with a **Keep my current plan** button.

## Billing details

The charge appears as a [subscription invoice](/help/billing/subscription-invoices). If the charge fails, see [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment). To switch between monthly and annual billing without changing plan, read [Annual or monthly billing](/help/billing/annual-vs-monthly-billing).
