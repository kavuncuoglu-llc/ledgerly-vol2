This page covers refunds of what you pay Ledgerly. To refund a payment your own client made, see [Refunds](/help/payments/refunds).

## Refund rules

| Charge | Refund |
| --- | --- |
| Annual plan | Full refund if requested within 30 days of purchase or renewal |
| Monthly plan | No refund for time already used |
| Upgrade proration | Not refunded separately; downgrade or cancel instead |
| Charged after cancelling in error | Refunded in full if you contact support within 14 days |
| Duplicate or incorrect charge | Always refunded |

Late payment is not part of this policy because Ledgerly does not charge late fees on subscriptions.

## How to request a refund

1. Open **Help > Contact support**.
2. Choose the category **Billing**.
3. Include the workspace name and the subscription invoice number, which starts with `LDG-`.

Support reviews requests on business days. See [Support hours and escalation](/help/troubleshooting/support-hours-and-escalation) for response times. Approved refunds go back to the original card within 5 to 10 business days.

## What happens to the workspace

After an annual refund, the workspace moves to read-only immediately, because the paid period no longer exists. Export your data first if you still need it (see [Export your data](/help/account/export-your-data)). Data is kept for 90 days.

## Refund compared with cancellation

Cancelling stops future charges but does not return money. A refund returns money for a period you have already paid. If you want both, request the refund and then cancel; see [Cancel your subscription](/help/billing/cancel-your-subscription).

## Taxes

Tax or VAT that was charged is refunded in proportion to the refunded amount. A corrected invoice, called a credit invoice, is issued with the `LDG-` prefix.
