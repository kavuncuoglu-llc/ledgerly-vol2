The payment method on file is used only for your Ledgerly subscription. It is not where your client payouts go. For payouts, see [Payout schedule](/help/payments/payout-schedule).

## Change the card

1. Go to **Settings > Subscription > Payment method**.
2. Choose **Update card**.
3. Enter the card number, expiry date, security code and billing postcode.
4. Choose **Save card**.

Ledgerly places a small temporary authorization on the card to check it works, and releases it within a few days. The new card is used from the next charge.

## Accepted methods

Visa, Mastercard and American Express credit and debit cards. Bank transfers are not available on Starter or Pro. Enterprise customers can pay by invoice with a purchase order by arrangement.

## Who can change it

The workspace Owner. Admins can see the last four digits of the card but cannot edit it.

## If the card is declined while saving

Check the number, the expiry date and the billing postcode, then try again. If the card is valid but still declined, your bank may be blocking the authorization. Try another card or ask the bank to allow it.

## If a payment is already failing

Updating the card does not charge it immediately unless a payment is overdue. If your subscription is in the retry cycle, choose **Retry payment now** after saving the card. See [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment).

## Receipts and billing email

To change the email address that receives receipts, edit the billing contacts under **Billing details** (see [Subscription invoices](/help/billing/subscription-invoices)).

## Security

Ledgerly does not store full card numbers. Card details are held by the payment processor. We will never ask you for your card number by email; see [Fake emails and phishing](/help/security/fake-emails-and-phishing).
