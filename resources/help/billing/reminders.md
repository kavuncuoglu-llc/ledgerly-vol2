title: Payment reminders
category: billing
summary: Describes the automatic reminder emails Ledgerly sends for unpaid invoices and when they go out.
updated: 2025-08-20
---

Ledgerly can email your client about an unpaid invoice without any action from you. Reminders are sent from your business name, include the invoice PDF link and a Pay now button, and are copied to every contact marked Receives invoices on the customer record.

## Automatic reminder schedule

Reminders are sent at 3, 7, and 14 days past due. The days are counted from the due date, so an invoice due on the 10th gets its first reminder on the 13th, the second on the 17th and the third on the 24th. All plans include this schedule.

| Reminder | Sent | Tone |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First | 3 days past due | Friendly nudge |
| Second | 7 days past due | Firm follow-up |
| Third | 14 days past due | Final notice before fees |

After the third reminder no further automatic emails go out. The late fee is added at 30 days past due (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)), and the client gets a separate late fee notice at that point. That notice is not a reminder and does not count toward the three.

Pro and Enterprise workspaces can change the schedule and the wording. See [Customize the reminder schedule](/help/reminders/customize-reminder-schedule) and [Reminder email templates](/help/reminders/reminder-email-templates).

## When reminders stop or pause

Reminders stop when the invoice becomes Paid or Void. They pause while the invoice is Disputed or a payment plan is active. During a plan, Ledgerly sends installment reminders 2 days before each installment instead.

If a client makes a partial payment, reminders continue and show the remaining balance.

## Reminders before the due date

An optional upcoming-due reminder can go out 3 days before the due date. It is off by default. See [Upcoming-due reminders](/help/reminders/upcoming-due-reminders).

## Sending one yourself

You can send a manual reminder at any time from the invoice page, once every 24 hours per invoice. See [Send a manual reminder](/help/reminders/send-a-manual-reminder). If a client says they never received an email, check [Reminder delivery problems](/help/reminders/reminder-delivery-problems).
