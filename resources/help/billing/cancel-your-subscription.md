You can cancel at any time. Only the workspace Owner can do it, and there is no cancellation fee.

## How to cancel

1. Open **Settings > Subscription**.
2. Choose **Cancel subscription**.
3. Pick a reason (optional) and confirm.

Cancelling stops renewal. Your plan stays active until the end of the period you already paid for, and the page shows the exact date. You can undo the cancellation before that date with **Resume subscription**.

## What happens when the plan ends

The workspace becomes read-only. You can still sign in, view and download invoices, export data and record payments you receive, but you cannot send new invoices or add customers. Recurring schedules are paused. Reminders and late fees for invoices that were already sent keep running, as they do for any read-only workspace. Existing pay links keep working, so clients can still pay.

Data is kept for 90 days after the end date. After that, the workspace and its data are permanently deleted. Export what you need first (see [Export your data](/help/account/export-your-data)). To delete the workspace immediately, see [Close your account](/help/account/close-your-account).

## Refunds

Monthly plans are not refunded for time already used. Annual plans get a full refund if you cancel within 30 days of purchase. Read [Subscription refunds](/help/billing/subscription-refunds).

## Pausing instead of cancelling

There is no pause option, but you can downgrade to Starter at the next renewal and keep a small set of features for $19 a month. See [Change your plan](/help/billing/change-your-plan).

## Several workspaces

Cancelling affects only the workspace you are viewing. Other workspaces keep their own subscriptions (see [Workspace billing](/help/billing/workspace-billing)).
