A dispute tells you that your client disagrees with an invoice: the amount, the work, or a fee added to it. Disputes are handled inside Ledgerly so that reminders and fees do not pile up while you sort things out.

## How a dispute starts

There are two ways to open one:

1. Your client opens the invoice link and chooses **Dispute this invoice**, then picks a reason and writes a short note.
2. You open the invoice, choose **More > Mark as disputed**, and record what the client told you by phone or email.

Either way the invoice gets a Disputed badge. The status itself does not change, so a Sent invoice stays Sent and an Overdue invoice stays Overdue. See [Invoice statuses](/help/invoices/statuses).

## What pauses during a dispute

While an invoice is Disputed:

- Automatic reminders stop (see [Payment reminders](/help/billing/reminders)).
- Late fees are not applied, and the days of the dispute are not charged later (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)).
- The client can still pay the full amount at any time, which closes the dispute.

You have 10 business days to respond. After that Ledgerly emails you a nudge, but it never closes a dispute on its own.

## Resolving a dispute

Choose **Resolve dispute** on the invoice and select an outcome:

- **Upheld**: you agree. Issue a [credit note](/help/invoices/credit-notes), edit the invoice or void it.
- **Rejected**: you do not agree. Add a reply for the client. Reminders and late fees resume from where they stopped.

## Late fee disputes

If a client disputes only a late fee, mark the invoice Disputed and then use **Waive late fee** or **Reduce late fee** on the fee line. Ledgerly does not remove fees automatically, and Ledgerly support cannot remove them for you because the invoice and the fee belong to your workspace. Details are in [Waive or reduce a late fee](/help/billing/waive-or-reduce-a-late-fee).

A client who disputes the fee because they believe they paid on time should be pointed to the payment record. If the payment is missing, record it using [Record an offline payment](/help/payments/record-an-offline-payment).

## Disputes and card chargebacks

A dispute in Ledgerly is not a chargeback. A chargeback is raised with the client's bank and is covered in [Chargebacks](/help/payments/chargebacks).
