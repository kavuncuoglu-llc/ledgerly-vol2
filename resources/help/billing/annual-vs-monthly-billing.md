You can pay for your Ledgerly subscription every month or once a year. The features of a plan are the same either way; only the billing rhythm and price differ.

## Comparison

| | Monthly | Annual |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Starter | $19 per month | $190 per year |
| Pro | $49 per month | $490 per year |
| Charged | Same day each month | Once, on the anniversary |
| Refund after purchase | None for time already used | Full refund within 30 days |

Annual billing works out to ten months for the price of twelve, a saving of two months. Enterprise pricing is agreed in your contract.

## Switching from monthly to annual

1. Open **Settings > Subscription**.
2. Choose **Switch to annual**.
3. Review the price. Ledgerly credits the unused part of the current month and charges the rest.

The change is immediate, and your renewal date becomes the day you switched.

## Switching from annual to monthly

Annual to monthly takes effect at the end of your paid year. Until then you keep the annual plan. A banner on the Subscription page lets you undo the change.

## Renewal and reminders

Annual plans renew automatically. Ledgerly emails the workspace Owner 14 days before the charge and again 3 days before it. If you do not want to renew, cancel before the renewal date (see [Cancel your subscription](/help/billing/cancel-your-subscription)).

## Things people often mix up

These charges are for your Ledgerly subscription only. They are separate from invoices you send to your clients, and they never include late fees. If a card payment on your subscription fails, the rules in [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment) apply, not the late fee rules in [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees).

For refunds on annual plans, see [Subscription refunds](/help/billing/subscription-refunds).
