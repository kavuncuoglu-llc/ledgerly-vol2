Ledgerly has three plans. Every plan includes invoicing, customers, payment reminders, late fees, the customer pay link and email support. You can change plans at any time.

## Prices

| | Starter | Pro | Enterprise |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Monthly | $19 | $49 | Custom |
| Annual | $190 | $490 | Custom |

Annual billing costs ten months for the price of twelve. See [Annual or monthly billing](/help/billing/annual-vs-monthly-billing). Prices are shown without tax. Tax or VAT is added at checkout where it applies.

## What each plan adds

**Starter** is for one person invoicing a handful of clients. It has one workspace, one team member, one currency and a fixed 1.5% late fee that you can switch on or off.

**Pro** adds team members, multiple currencies and automation: customizable reminders, customer statements, the API and webhooks, accounting sync, bank feeds and sending from your own domain. The late fee stays at 1.5%.

**Enterprise** adds single sign-on, a custom late fee rate and grace period, per-customer reminder rules, unlimited team members and a faster support response. Pricing depends on volume; contact support@example.com.

Exact numbers such as customers, invoices per month and recurring schedules are in [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits).

## Free trial

New workspaces start with a 14-day trial of Pro. No card is required. At the end of the trial you choose a plan or the workspace becomes read-only. See [Free trial](/help/getting-started/free-trial).

## Payment processing

Plan prices do not include card or bank transfer fees on client payments. Those are listed in [Payment processing fees](/help/payments/payment-processing-fees).
