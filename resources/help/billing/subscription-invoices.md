Ledgerly invoices you for your subscription in the same way you invoice your clients. These invoices are separate from the invoices inside your workspace, so they do not appear in your Invoices list and do not count toward your invoice limit.

## Where to find them

Open **Settings > Subscription > Billing history**. Each row shows the date, plan, amount, tax and a **Download PDF** link. A copy is also emailed to the billing contact on the day of every charge. Subscription invoice numbers start with `LDG-` so they are easy to tell apart from your own `INV-` numbers.

## Changing the details printed on them

Edit the billing name, address, billing email and tax or VAT ID under **Settings > Subscription > Billing details**. The new details appear on future invoices. Past subscription invoices cannot be reissued with new details, but support can send a corrected copy of any invoice from the last 12 months on request.

Make sure your tax ID is entered before your next charge if you need it on the invoice. Ledgerly cannot add a tax ID to an invoice after it has been issued.

## Taxes on subscription invoices

Where tax or VAT applies, it is added on top of the plan price shown on [Plans and pricing](/help/billing/plans-and-pricing). Businesses outside the sales tax region with a valid VAT ID may be charged under the reverse charge rules.

## Who receives them

By default the workspace Owner. You can add up to three extra billing contacts, who get the same email. Billing contacts do not need a Ledgerly login.

## Due dates and late payment

A subscription invoice is due on the day it is issued, because the card on file is charged automatically. There is no grace period and no late fee on subscription charges. If a charge fails, the retry rules in [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment) apply.

## Several workspaces

Each workspace has its own subscription and its own list of subscription invoices. See [Workspace billing](/help/billing/workspace-billing).
