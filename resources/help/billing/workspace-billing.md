In Ledgerly, billing belongs to a workspace, not to a person. A workspace is the container for one business: its customers, invoices, settings and team. If you run more than one business, you can have several workspaces, and each is billed separately.

## What is billed per workspace

- The plan and its price. One workspace can be on Pro while another is on Starter.
- The plan limits, such as 500 customers on Pro. Limits are not shared across workspaces (see [Plan limits](/help/billing/plan-limits)).
- The payment method, billing contacts and tax ID.
- The subscription invoices (see [Subscription invoices](/help/billing/subscription-invoices)).

Your personal profile and sign-in are shared across all workspaces, so there is no extra login.

## Workspaces per plan

Starter includes 1 workspace, Pro includes up to 3 and Enterprise has no limit. To add a workspace go to **Account menu > New workspace**. The 14-day Pro trial is given once per account, so additional workspaces start on Starter until you upgrade them.

## Switching between workspaces

Use the switcher at the top left. The Subscription page always shows the plan for the workspace you are currently in. This is a common source of confusion: if you upgraded one workspace and a feature is still missing, check which workspace you are viewing.

## Moving the bill to someone else

The Owner of a workspace pays for it. To change the Owner, see [Team roles and permissions](/help/account/team-roles-and-permissions). The new Owner will need to add their own payment method.

## What happens to a client-facing invoice if the subscription lapses

If the subscription lapses, existing invoices stay visible to clients and can still be paid. See [Failed subscription payment](/help/billing/failed-subscription-payment) for the timeline.

For the difference between a workspace and an account, read [Workspaces](/help/account/workspaces).
