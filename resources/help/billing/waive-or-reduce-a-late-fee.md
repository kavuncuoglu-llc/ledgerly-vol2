title: Waive or reduce a late fee

Ledgerly never waives a late fee on its own, not even the first one a client incurs. Because the fee is part of your invoice, you decide whether to keep it. Owners, Admins and Billers can remove or lower a fee at any time.

## Waive a late fee

1. Open the Overdue invoice.
2. On the **Late fee** line, choose **Waive**.
3. Pick a reason (goodwill, billing error, agreement with client or other) and add an optional note.
4. Confirm.

The fee line is removed, the invoice total drops by that amount and the balance is recalculated. A waiver stops all future fees on that invoice too, so the client is not charged again the next night. The reason is saved in the invoice history and is not shown to the client.

## Reduce a late fee

Choose **Reduce** instead and enter either a new amount or a percentage of the current fee. Ledgerly keeps the reduced amount as a fixed fee. It stops growing, so a reduced fee does not increase by 0.05% a day. If you only want to forgive part of the time, reduce the fee to the amount you are comfortable with.

## What a waiver does not do

- It does not change the invoice status. An unpaid invoice stays Overdue (see [Invoice statuses](/help/invoices/statuses)).
- It does not refund money. If the client already paid the fee, you will need to refund it as described in [Refunds](/help/payments/refunds).
- It does not turn off late fees for other invoices. To do that, see [Late fee settings](/help/billing/late-fee-settings).

## Who can ask Ledgerly to remove a fee

Support cannot remove a late fee from your client's invoice, since it is your charge to your client. If you are the client and received an invoice from a business that uses Ledgerly, contact that business. Ledgerly staff can see your invoice but cannot change the amount.

## Related reading

If the client says the fee should never have applied, mark the invoice as disputed first. See [Disputing a charge](/help/billing/disputes). To understand how the fee was calculated before you waive it, read [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees).
