This page is about the charge for your own Ledgerly subscription. If a payment from one of your clients fails, see [Failed payments](/help/payments/failed-payments) instead.

## What Ledgerly does after a decline

1. **Day 0**: the charge is declined. The Owner and billing contacts get an email, and a banner appears at the top of the workspace.
2. **Days 3, 5 and 7**: Ledgerly retries the card automatically and emails you after each failed attempt.
3. **Day 14**: if the payment has still not gone through, the workspace becomes read-only.

Read-only means you can sign in, view and export everything, and record payments, but you cannot send invoices, run recurring schedules or add customers. Recurring schedules keep their place and create drafts after the workspace is restored. Reminders and late fees for invoices that were already sent continue to run in read-only mode, so your clients are still chased.

Your data is kept. Ledgerly does not delete anything because of a failed payment.

## No late fee on your subscription

Ledgerly does not charge a late fee on subscription payments. Late fees apply only to invoices that you send to your clients (see [Late fees](/help/billing/late-fees)).

## How to fix it

1. Go to **Settings > Subscription > Payment method**.
2. Choose **Update card** and enter a working card (see [Update your payment method](/help/billing/update-payment-method)).
3. Choose **Retry payment now**.

If the payment succeeds, the workspace is unlocked within a minute.

## Common reasons for a decline

- The card expired or was replaced by your bank.
- Your bank blocked the amount as unusual. Ask the bank to approve charges from Ledgerly.
- Insufficient funds.
- The billing country does not match the card.

If you believe you were charged incorrectly or the retries did not behave as described, open a ticket from **Help > Contact support** (see [Contact support](/help/troubleshooting/contact-support)).
